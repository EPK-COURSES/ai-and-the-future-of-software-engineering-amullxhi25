# References

All sources used in this research are listed below. Each reference corresponds to statistics, findings, or analysis cited in research.md.

-----

## 1. Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2024

- **Source:** Stack Overflow
- **URL:** <https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/>
- **Description:** Annual developer survey with over 65,000 responses from 185+ countries. Used for statistics on AI adoption rates, developer usage of AI tools, and developer sentiment toward AI in 2024.

-----

## 2. Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025

- **Source:** Stack Overflow
- **URL:** <https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai/>
- **Description:** Annual developer survey with over 49,000 responses from 177 countries. Used for 2025 statistics on AI adoption (84%), daily AI usage (51%), trust in AI output, and developer resistance to AI in high-responsibility tasks.
